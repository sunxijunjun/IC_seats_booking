# IC Seat Booking: AIR Lab, Room W402z2

Seats in Room W402z2 of the AIR Lab can be booked by PolyU students and staff.
A seat can be used for a single session or regularly over a longer period.
The room is also used for classes, so bookings should avoid class times.

## Seat availability

**Live availability page:** https://sunxijunjun.github.io/IC_seats_booking/

No budget is available for a domain name.
For this reason, the seat availability is pushed to this static page at regular times for your reference.
The page shows the next 7 days and cannot take bookings.

The page is updated in two cases:

- When a booking is made, changed, or cancelled. Updates are pushed at most once every 7 minutes.
- At the end of each session (12:30 / 17:30 / 22:30) and at midnight.

The time of the last update is shown at the top of the page.
A new booking may take about 8 minutes to appear.
If an old version is shown, the page can be reloaded with Ctrl+Shift+R.

## How to book

Bookings are made through Microsoft Forms.
The **Book with Microsoft Forms** button on the availability page opens the form.
A confirmation email is sent after the booking is processed.

- **Room:** 30 seats at 10 tables, T01 to T10. Each table has seats A to C from left to right.
- **Sessions:** 08:30-12:30 / 12:30-17:30 / 17:30-22:30, or the full day.
- **Booking window:** today and the next 6 days. Regular users need to book again each week.
- **Who can book:** holders of a PolyU email address ending in @polyu.edu.hk or @connect.polyu.hk.
- **Today:** a session cannot be booked after it ends. A full-day booking for today can be made at any time.

## Class times

The room is closed to bookings during a whole session that is used for a class.
These cells are grey on the availability page.

A class may also use only part of a session.
Such a session can still be booked, but the seat can only be used outside the class time.
The page and the confirmation email both list the class time and the free time, for example:

> 08:30-12:30 session: class 08:30-11:30. You can use your seat 11:30-12:30.

## Contact

To change or cancel a booking, or for any question, please contact the IC staff in Room W402d next door.
